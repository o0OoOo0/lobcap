# Simulation workbench review

The application now keeps strategy editing on the left and immutable results on
the right. Each policy explains its decision clock, trigger, order behavior, and
limitations. A submitted configuration includes numerical examples generated by
the same policy functions as the replay engine. These are labeled hypothetical
states, not sample predictions. Editing or navigating never submits a run.

## What was deficient

- Settings and outcomes were separated across pages; the time path was hidden.
- Tiny tick distances and signals were easy to misinterpret as economically large
  changes. Tick rounding and the BBO price clamp can erase those changes.
- Baseline execution policies looked like validated trading strategies. None of
  these formulas is a calibrated source of alpha or an established paper candidate.
- A single final PnL obscured open inventory and adverse fill selection.

The main plot now aligns net/gross PnL, spread contribution, inventory MTM,
inventory, midpoint, decision targets, and actual simulated fills on UTC time.
Decision targets are explicitly distinct from active orders. The order tab retains
source-linked lifecycle evidence, stage changes, and markout coverage.

## Metric review

Canonical accounting is unchanged: spread capture + inventory MTM = gross PnL;
gross PnL - signed fees = net PnL. Every ledger transition checks these identities.
The display independently matches fills FIFO, including long/short inventory,
partial closes, position reversal, fees/rebates, and initial inventory at the sample
starting midpoint. Realized + unrealized must equal gross, and reconstructed ending
inventory must match the ledger. This decomposition is not added to the original
attribution. The view flags inconsistencies rather than changing stored artifacts.

Maximum drawdown uses event-time equity, not the 1-second plotted sample. Order
fill rate and quantity fill rate have different denominators. Nominal turnover
counts both buys and sells. Break-even fee is gross PnL / turnover * 10,000 for an
unchanged fill path, not a prediction for fee-dependent policies. Initial equity
return depends on arbitrary synthetic cash. Ten minutes cannot support BRAIN-style
Sharpe, Fitness, annualization, or claims of out-of-sample quality.

## Modeling boundaries checked against primary documentation

[HftBacktest order fills](https://hftbacktest.readthedocs.io/en/latest/order_fill.html)
describes the limits of unmodified historical replay and modeled queue position
when only market-by-price data is available. Our participation budget does not
measure market impact; hypothetical cost stress must not be called endogenous
impact. [HftBacktest latency models](https://hftbacktest.readthedocs.io/en/latest/latency_models.html)
distinguishes feed, entry, and response latency. The selected clock and all three
configured execution delays remain visible per run. External adapter parity is
not established by passing this repository's tests.

Next research step: fix a hypothesis and execution assumptions, vary one active
parameter, inspect changed order behavior and exposure, then require fresh data
before selecting a paper-trading candidate. Do not tune alpha or sample bounds to
manufacture profit.

The 100-tick result ends at +0.01 BTC (the inventory limit) and has 1.0701 USDT
maximum drawdown. Its positive total depends on open inventory valuation; it is
not evidence of reliable spread harvesting. The unchanged-fill-path break-even
average fee is 1.4199 bps. BBO's gross decomposition is -0.02885 spread capture
and -1.2772 inventory MTM, with negative post-fill drift at 100 ms, 1 s and 5 s.
The loss is not an arithmetic rule forcing all strategies negative: a controlled
profitable passive-fill fixture passes, and sufficiently different quote distances
change actual fills. Neither observation establishes execution-model calibration.
